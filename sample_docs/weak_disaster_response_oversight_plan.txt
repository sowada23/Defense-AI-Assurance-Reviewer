Expected Quality: Weak
Title: Disaster Response AI Oversight Draft

1. System Purpose
The draft says an AI assistant will help with disaster response coordination. It does not define what the tool is allowed to do or what it must not do.

2. Intended Users
The draft says staff will use it during emergencies. Roles, training, and approval authority are not defined.

3. Data Sources
The assistant will use reports and messages from several places. The draft does not document data sources, quality checks, retention, or redaction.

4. Model or System Evaluation
The draft says the assistant gave good summaries in a demo. It provides no metrics, no validation set, and no stress testing.

5. Human Oversight
The draft says people will remain involved but gives no review, override, escalation, or audit procedure.

6. Failure Modes
The draft does not analyze failure modes such as outdated information, duplicate requests, mistranslation, or missing data.

7. Risk Mitigations
No concrete mitigations are listed.

8. Privacy and Security Considerations
Privacy and security controls are not described.

9. Deployment Readiness
The draft suggests the tool is ready after a demo. It has no readiness criteria, pilot, training, monitoring, or rollback plan.

10. Known Limitations
The limitations section is empty.
